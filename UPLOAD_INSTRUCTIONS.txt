WHAT v1.3 DOES
--------------
Forward Validation is intentionally different from the historical labs.

When you click "Arm new forward cohort":
1. Deriv Insight asks Deriv for the latest tick on each selected 1-second
   Volatility market.
2. That tick epoch is saved as the market's immutable anchor.
3. Window 1 starts on anchor_epoch + 1.
4. No tick at or before the anchor can enter that forward window.

Each window is frozen at exactly 25,000 consecutive 1-second epochs.

For a 1-second market:
25,000 ticks ≈ 6 hours 56 minutes 40 seconds.

The next window starts one epoch after the previous window's end, so windows
do not overlap.

COLLECTING A WINDOW
-------------------
Open the cohort and click:

    Check / collect next forward window

The browser checks one market per HTTP request.

For each market:
- If fewer than 25,000 future seconds have elapsed, status = WAITING.
- If ready, the server requests the explicit start/end interval from Deriv in
  <=900-second chunks.
- It requires exactly 25,000 unique future tick epochs.
- If the integrity count is wrong, NOTHING is saved.
- If valid, the window is analyzed and saved.

This avoids both:
- the Gunicorn timeout from v1.2.2, and
- accidental reuse of pre-arm historical ticks.

FROZEN RESEARCH FAMILY
----------------------
The statistical family is unchanged:
- Even
- Odd
- Over 4
- Under 5

Frozen gate:
- 25,000 future ticks
- 70% chronological discovery
- 30% untouched holdout
- minimum context N = 150
- minimum discovery uplift = +0.75 percentage points
- Benjamini-Hochberg FDR q <= 0.10
- shrinkage = 200
- evaluation-time live price check

A live RESEARCH CANDIDATE still requires:
- holdout validation;
- shrunk holdout P > current break-even;
- raw Wilson 95% lower bound > current break-even.

DATA INTEGRITY
--------------
Each completed window stores:
- exact start epoch;
- exact end epoch;
- tick count;
- pip size;
- SHA-256 fingerprint of epoch + raw price sequence;
- test counts;
- candidate counts;
- baseline economics;
- validated candidate summaries.

The raw 25,000 prices are not permanently duplicated in the database.

REPEATED FORWARD EFFECTS
------------------------
The Forward Validation page reports an exact context + outcome pair only when
it independently survives holdout in at least TWO future windows.

No demo or real execution is enabled.

FIRST USE AFTER DEPLOYMENT
--------------------------
1. Open "Forward Validation Lab".
2. Leave all active 1-second Volatility markets selected.
3. Stake = $1.
4. Give the cohort a name, e.g.:
       Forward Cohort 1
5. Click "Arm new forward cohort".
6. Confirm every market shows its arm boundary and W1.
7. Immediately clicking "Check / collect..." should normally show WAITING.
   That is correct: v1.3 refuses to backfill old ticks.
8. Return after at least ~6h 57m and click "Check / collect next forward
   window".
9. The markets are checked one at a time and completed windows are saved.
