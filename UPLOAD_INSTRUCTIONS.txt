WHAT CHANGES
------------
Balanced Contracts Lab is now multi-market.

When the page opens, currently active 1-second Volatility markets are selected
by default, up to eight:
- 1HZ10V
- 1HZ15V
- 1HZ25V
- 1HZ30V
- 1HZ50V
- 1HZ75V
- 1HZ90V
- 1HZ100V
(only symbols actually returned by Deriv are shown/selected)

You can select up to eight markets and click:

RUN ALL BALANCED STUDIES

Each selected market gets:
- 25,000 ticks (or 10,000 if chosen)
- 70% discovery / 30% holdout
- four pre-declared outcomes only:
  * Even
  * Odd
  * Over 4
  * Under 5
- min context N 150
- min discovery uplift +0.75 pp
- BH-FDR q <= 0.10
- live ask/payout check

The consolidated comparison shows:
- Even probability / live break-even
- Odd probability / live break-even
- Over 4 probability / live break-even
- Under 5 probability / live break-even
- tests
- discovery candidates
- validated candidates
- live research candidates

RESUMABLE
---------
Every market is saved immediately in the existing ResearchRun table.

If any market fails after the v1.2.1 rate-limit retries, the page shows:

RETRY FAILED MARKETS

That button reruns only failed markets for that saved batch.
Completed markets are not repeated.

CROSS-MARKET RECURRENCE
-----------------------
If the exact same condition/outcome pair independently survives holdout on two
or more markets, the page reports it in a separate recurrence table.

A recurring validated condition is still research evidence, not an instruction
to trade.
