PART A — RATE LIMIT / RESUMABLE BATCHES
---------------------------------------
The public client now:
- keeps one WebSocket open for historical paging whenever possible;
- deliberately paces 1,000-tick history pages;
- catches HTTP/WebSocket 429 RateLimit responses;
- honors Retry-After when websocket-client exposes it;
- otherwise uses bounded exponential backoff with jitter;
- reconnects and retries the SAME historical page rather than restarting the
  whole 25,000-tick history request;
- retries transient WebSocket timeouts/transport failures.

The Cross-Market page now:
- gives every batch a saved batch ID;
- saves each market's COMPLETE or FAILED state immediately in ResearchRun;
- exposes "Retry failed markets" for incomplete batches;
- does not rerun markets already completed in that batch;
- also offers the latest incomplete batch again after a later page reload.

Routine web quote archiving is narrowed to:
- Even
- Odd
- Over 4
- Under 5
This lowers proposal traffic.

PART B — BALANCED CONTRACTS LAB
--------------------------------
New menu:
Balanced Contracts Lab

The four pre-declared contracts are:
- DIGITEVEN
- DIGITODD
- DIGITOVER barrier 4
- DIGITUNDER barrier 5

The statistical gate is frozen:
- 70% chronological discovery
- 30% holdout
- minimum context N = 150
- minimum discovery uplift = +0.75 percentage points
- Benjamini-Hochberg FDR q <= 0.10
- shrinkage = 200

The page shows:
1. Holdout-wide baseline probability for each of the four contracts.
2. Wilson 95% lower confidence bound.
3. Current ask / payout / break-even probability.
4. Baseline edge versus the live price.
5. Conditional discovery candidates.
6. Holdout validation.
7. Shrunk conditional probability.
8. Live-price model edge and lower-confidence edge.

"RESEARCH CANDIDATE" requires:
- the condition survived discovery and holdout;
- shrunk holdout probability exceeds current break-even;
- raw holdout 95% lower bound also exceeds current break-even.

It is still research only. It does NOT execute demo or real contracts.
