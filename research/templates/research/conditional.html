What is being tested?

The scan includes previous digit, previous parity, high/low digit band, exact two-digit sequences, two-step parity, repeated-vs-different state, three-step parity and three-identical-digit streaks. Each condition is evaluated across Digit Match, Differs, Over, Under, Even and Odd outcomes. Exact two-digit patterns are only considered when they meet the minimum observation rule.
